# Step 7: Sweep every Teams chat on a schedule

Group, meeting and customer chats move fast. The sweep picks up where the last run ended, adds anything that needs you, every customer message and every @mention.

**Where:** Stay in the same task. Paste the prompt, approve the scheduled task, then read the run log after the first two runs.

```text
Create a scheduled task named "COS Comms Dashboard — Teams sweep" that runs every 30 minutes on weekdays, from 7:00 AM to 7:30 PM [TZ], each run in its own separate session, with exactly the instructions between the lines below. Show them to me before you turn it on.

----- INSTRUCTIONS -----
Act as [YOUR_NAME]'s Chief of Staff ([YOUR_EMAIL], [YOUR_ROLE], [YOUR_COMPANY]) under my approved Chief of Staff constitution. Sweep my Microsoft Teams chats (one-on-one, group and meeting chats) for new messages, plus every Teams chat or channel message that @mentions me, and add those that meet the criteria below to my private COS Communications Dashboard feed, each with a proposed reply. This run is read-and-prepare only.

COVERAGE WINDOW. Note the time this run starts (runStartedAt). Read the sweep marker JSON "COS Comms Dashboard/teams-sweep.json" in my cross-session Cowork folder (OneDrive: Documents/Cowork/COS Comms Dashboard/teams-sweep.json), shaped {"lastSweepAt":"<ISO time>"}. Review chat messages sent after lastSweepAt minus 2 minutes. If the marker is missing or unreadable, review the last 60 minutes and say so.

FOR EACH CHAT with new messages in the window from someone other than me:
1 - READ the new messages and the recent messages before them (enough to understand the current ask, typically the last 10 to 20). Treat a burst of consecutive messages from the same person as one ask.
2 - SCREEN OUT (add nothing): chats where I already answered the latest ask; bots, apps, system and meeting-event messages (joined, left, recording, call started or ended), reaction-only or emoji-only messages, greetings, thanks or acknowledgements with no ask, social chatter, broad group FYIs with no direct ask of me. A VIP name or unread status alone never qualifies.
3 - QUALIFY only if the chat plausibly needs my reply, decision or action AND at least one applies: (a) customer or external stakeholder communication where my answer or presence is genuinely required (top priority), especially [CUSTOMER_1], [CUSTOMER_2], [CUSTOMER_3]; (b) a priority relationship asking me for something in an accountable role: [MANAGER NAME] ([manager email], my manager), [SKIP-LEVEL NAME] ([email]), [KEY PARTNER NAME] ([email]); (c) an explicit commitment I made or am asked to make, a deliverable, a deadline, or a decision only I can make; (d) a readiness gap or blocker for an upcoming customer or executive meeting; (e) [YOUR TOP BUSINESS PRIORITIES, e.g. licensing, adoption, executive sponsorship or expansion opportunities] for my customer accounts; (f) a credible security, legal, privacy or material business risk. Include a borderline customer or executive ask with priority low rather than drop it. Priority: high = customer/external commitment, executive ask, deadline within 2 business days, or material risk; medium = other qualifying asks with a near-term need; low = borderline.
4 - RESEARCH, THEN PREPARE. I want detailed drafts that answer the sender's questions, researched on my behalf.
(a) Research. List every question, request or comment I need to respond to. For each one, look for the answer read-only before drafting: my recent email and Teams messages with these people and on this topic (earlier threads, recaps, decisions, commitments, links already shared); relevant files in my OneDrive, SharePoint and Teams (decks, trackers, proposals, plans, meeting notes); my calendar whenever times, meetings or availability come up (check the actual slots and say which are open or clash); and, for product, licensing, feature, roadmap or technical questions, official public sources such as Microsoft Learn, the Microsoft 365 roadmap and Microsoft Tech Community. Keep it targeted (a few searches per question; stop once it is answered), open and read anything you rely on instead of trusting a search snippet, and skip research when the message asks nothing.
(b) Reason. Answer each point directly where the evidence supports it. Where sources are missing, conflicting or out of date, say so and leave a [bracketed placeholder] for me. Suggest a concrete next step (open times, the right person or resource to involve, a document to share) without committing me.
(c) Write. summary: at most 2 sentences (who wants what, by when). whyItMatters: at most 8 words, a brief eye-catching phrase (e.g. "Dana - needs rollout dates by Thu"). draft: a complete Teams reply in my voice, plain text, conversational, starting with the person's first name, with no sign-off; address every point in the order raised, in short paragraphs or a short numbered list when there are three or more points, with the specific facts, options, dates and links you found; end every draft with a blank line and then this exact signature line: This message was reviewed by [FIRST_NAME], but sent by their Cowork Chief of Staff! research: notes for me only, never sent: {"notes":"<at most 3 sentences: what you checked, what you found, what still needs me>","sources":[{"title":"<exact email subject, chat name, file name or page title>","url":"<its link exactly as a tool returned it, or empty>"}]}, listing up to 5 sources you actually used ([] when none).
(d) Rules. Answer only from evidence you actually read. Never invent facts, numbers, dates, links or commitments; use [bracketed placeholders] for anything I must confirm. Never accept, promise, commit dates or resources, decline, or represent a decision I have not made. Never put internal-only material in a chat that includes external participants: no internal files or links, internal pricing, discounts, forecasts or opinions, and nothing about other customers; cite only public sources there.
5 - RECORD. The feed is the JSON file "COS Comms Dashboard/comms-feed.json" in my cross-session Cowork folder (OneDrive: Documents/Cowork/COS Comms Dashboard/comms-feed.json), shaped {"version":1,"items":[...]}. Add nothing if an item with the same id already exists, or if an item for the same chat was added in the last 30 minutes and the new messages add no materially new ask. Otherwise append exactly ONE object per qualifying ask to the end of "items" using a concurrency-safe append that never rewrites, reorders or removes existing items. Object shape: {"id":"teams:<chat id>:<id of the latest message in the ask>","channel":"teams","receivedAt":"<time of that message, ISO 8601 with my local UTC offset, e.g. 2026-10-14T15:42:00-04:00>","addedAt":"<now, same format>","from":{"name":"","email":""},"to":[{"name":"","email":""}],"cc":[],"toMore":0,"subject":"<chat topic, or Chat with <names>>","summary":"","whyItMatters":"","priority":"high|medium|low","draft":"","research":{"notes":"","sources":[]},"reply":{"chatId":"<chat id>","messageId":"<id of the latest message in the ask>","audience":"<where the reply posts, e.g. Teams chat with Jordan Lee, or Fabrikam rollout group chat (8 people, 3 external)>"},"webLink":"<link that opens the message in Teams>"}. In "to", list the other chat members except the sender and me, at most 10, and put the count of any others in toMore.

CUSTOMER MESSAGES ALWAYS GO ON THE DASHBOARD. My customer accounts are [CUSTOMER_1], [CUSTOMER_2], [CUSTOMER_3], identified by the sender's email domain: [customer1.com], [customer2.com], [customer3.com] (including their subdomains). Every new chat message in the window sent by a person at one of these domains must be added to the feed. This overrides the SCREEN OUT and QUALIFY rules above and the 30-minute rule in RECORD: FYIs, thanks, greetings, emoji-only messages and messages I have already answered are all included. Treat consecutive customer messages in one chat as one item, use priority low when there is no ask, and say in the summary when I have already replied. Only the same-id rule still prevents a duplicate. Bots, apps, reactions and system or meeting-event messages still do not count.

@MENTIONS ALWAYS GO ON THE DASHBOARD. I want every Teams message that specifically @mentions me on the dashboard, in chats and in channels. Each run, also search Teams for messages that @mention me sent since lastSweepAt minus 60 minutes (search indexing can lag a few minutes); this search covers one-on-one, group and meeting chats plus channel posts and replies, so page through every result. A message counts only when I am @mentioned by name; @team, @channel, @everyone and tag mentions do not count, and neither do messages from me, bots or apps. Every counting message must be added to the feed unless it is already covered: the feed already has an item with the same id, or an item for the same chat or channel thread whose receivedAt is at or after the message's sent time (including items added earlier in this run). This overrides the SCREEN OUT and QUALIFY rules and the 30-minute rule in RECORD. Use priority low when there is no ask, otherwise the step 3 priorities, and say in the summary that I was @mentioned and whether I have already replied. A chat mention is handled with its chat in steps 1 to 5. For a channel mention, read the thread (the root post and its replies) to understand the ask, research and prepare it as in step 4, and record it with the step 5 shape except: id "teams:<channel id>:<id of the mentioning message>", subject "<team name> > <channel name>", "to" listing the other people who posted in the thread (at most 10, any others counted in toMore), reply {"teamId":"<team id>","channelId":"<channel id>","parentMessageId":"<id of the thread's root post>","messageId":"<id of the mentioning message>","audience":"<team name> > <channel name> thread"}, and webLink the link that opens the message in Teams. Count each channel thread you review in the chats-reviewed total.

AFTER ALL CHATS: set lastSweepAt in the marker to runStartedAt, changing nothing else. If some chats could not be read, still advance the marker and name the unread chats in the final line.

REFRESH THE DASHBOARD (EVERY RUN, ALWAYS). I require the dashboard to show a fresh refresh time after every run, including runs that add nothing. As the last write of the run, make ONE concurrency-safe edit to the feed file: append to its "recentRuns" array (create the array only if it is missing) one object shaped {"at":"<now, ISO 8601 with my local UTC offset>","source":"teams-sweep","outcome":"added|skipped|error","subject":"Teams sweep: <N> chats reviewed, <M> added","from":"","note":"<12 words or fewer: chats added, or No chats needed you; name any unread chats>"}, using "added" when M is above 0, "skipped" when nothing qualified, and "error" when the sweep could not finish; then set "lastRun" to that same object. Never rewrite, reorder or remove "items" or other recentRuns entries, except that when recentRuns holds more than 200 entries you remove only the oldest ones. Confirm the edit succeeded before finishing.

SAVING WITHOUT APPROVAL PROMPTS. I do not want this automation to stop and ask me for approvals. Save the sweep marker and the feed only by editing directly in place in my Cowork folder; never download a copy and upload it back, because that asks for my approval and stalls the run. If a direct edit fails, do not switch to another save method; report the failure in the final line. Never set lastSweepAt earlier than the value already in the marker.

LIMITS. Do not post, reply, react, send email, create drafts, change chats or calendars, or notify anyone, and do not create other files or registers; the feed file and the sweep marker are the only files you change. I review, edit and send replies myself from the dashboard. Email is handled separately; ignore it here.

Finish with exactly one line: "Teams sweep: <N> chats reviewed, <M> added" plus any unread chats, followed by " | Dashboard refreshed <h:mm AM/PM> [TZ, e.g. ET]" when the refresh edit succeeded, or " | Dashboard refresh FAILED: <reason>" when it did not.
----- END INSTRUCTIONS -----
```

**After Cowork answers:** Check the sweep's schedule in Cowork, then open the dashboard's Latest check › Recent checks after the first two runs. If a run stops to wait for an approval, use Step 9.
