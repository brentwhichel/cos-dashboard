How it works

Watch, store, act

Cowork automations watch your mail and Teams and write a private feed to your OneDrive. This dashboard reads that feed. You stay in control of every reply and every commitment.

Watch · Cowork automations

  • Inbox email screening
  • Customer email screening
  • Key-person chat alerts
  • Scheduled Teams sweep

Store · private feed in OneDrive

  • One item per message that needs you
  • Priority and why it matters
  • Researched draft, reply audience, sources
  • Run log and Last refreshed

Act · this dashboard

  • Review by day, newest first
  • Edit the draft, then Send (with a 5-second undo)
  • Mark complete, reopen or delete
  • My tasks, on the same page

Lessons from the live build

Know these before you demo

  • !Editing pauses automations. After any change, re-activate the automation in Cowork Automations. The dashboard warns you when no check has landed for two business hours.
  • !Some external replies need Outlook. Your organization may block apps from emailing people outside the company. If Send is blocked, the draft is unchanged: send that reply from Outlook.
  • !Check the reply audience. A reply to a forwarded invite or an alert mailbox can go to the wrong person. When the summary says so, reply in the Teams chat instead.
  • !Save files directly. A run that saves by downloading and re-uploading waits for an approval nobody sees. The instructions here always edit files in place.
  • !Keep one feed. If OneDrive shows "conflicted" copies of comms-feed.json, ask Cowork to fold anything missing back into the main file, then delete the copies.
  • !Watch your credits. Every run uses Copilot Credits, and most runs are email screenings that end in a skip. Outlook rules that keep marketing and newsletters out of your Inbox, or an hourly Teams sweep, cut runs the most. Type /cost in a Cowork task to see your usage.

Troubleshooting

Common questions

The dashboard says "Couldn't find your feed". Step 2 hasn't run yet, or you picked the wrong folder. Pick Documents › Cowork › COS Comms Dashboard in your synced OneDrive.

"No checks recorded since..." An automation is paused (often after an edit) or waiting for an approval. Open Cowork Automations, re-activate it, and approve any pending request.

I don't see the OneDrive folder on my computer. Open OneDrive in the browser, go to Documents › Cowork, select the folder and choose Sync, or use Microsoft 365 sign-in instead.

Can other people see my dashboard? No. The page has no server. It reads your own files in your browser, and the website owner never sees them.
